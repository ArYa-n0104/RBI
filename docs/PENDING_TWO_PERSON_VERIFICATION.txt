------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

This project's own rules (docs/ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL.md and
docs/DATASET_SPEC.md) say a test question can only be called
"gold_verified" -- a real, trustworthy answer key entry -- after:

  1. Two DIFFERENT people each answer the question on their own,
     without seeing the other person's answer.
  2. Any disagreement between them is resolved by actually checking
     the official RBI/SEBI text and writing down why one answer is
     right.
  3. The clause's source link, exact wording, and effective date are
     also checked.

This is called "dual annotation + adjudication" in the project docs.
It is NOT the same as one person checking something carefully, even
twice. The whole point is that a second, independent person can catch
a mistake the first person did not notice.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THIS MATTERS -- WHAT DEPENDS ON IT
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Nothing else in the project is blocked by this being open. But a
few specific things ARE blocked, and should not be attempted or
claimed until it is resolved:

1. No item can be marked "gold_verified."
   The dataset validator (src/taarr/dataset.py) will not accept that
   label without two recorded annotator outcomes plus an adjudication
   record. This is enforced automatically, not just a guideline.

2. No real accuracy, F1, or agreement (Cohen's kappa) numbers exist.
   src/taarr/evaluate.py, experiments.py, and the benchmark-run API
   endpoint only ever score rows with annotation_status ==
   "gold_verified." With zero such rows, every metric is reported as
   "null / not available," on purpose, instead of a fake zero or a
   number based on unverified data.

3. Evaluation against other methods (dense search, graph-based search,
   etc.) cannot produce a real, defensible result yet.
   It is possible to run those comparisons against the
   "source_text_human_confirmed" tier right now, but any such numbers
   must be clearly labeled "illustrative, not the final verified
   result" in any report or paper draft -- never presented as the real
   evaluation.

4. The paper's results/evaluation section cannot be written with real
   numbers until the step above produces them.

In short: Phase 4 (dataset) has made real progress today -- the source
rules are now human-confirmed -- but still cannot be marked fully done,
and Phase 5 (evaluation) still cannot produce trustworthy final
numbers, until a second independent person checks the benchmark
questions.

  - The project owner does a second, separate pass over a sample of

  - Any other genuinely independent second reviewer is found.

Until then: this file exists so nobody forgets this gap is open, and
so nobody accidentally writes "verified" or "gold" results into the
paper that the data does not actually support yet.
