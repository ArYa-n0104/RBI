------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

This project's own rules (docs/ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL.md and
docs/DATASET_SPEC.md) say a test question can only be called
"gold_verified" -- a real, trustworthy answer key entry -- after:

  1. Two DIFFERENT people each answer the question on their own,
     without seeing the other person's answer.
  2. Any disagreement between them is resolved by actually checking
     the official RBI/SEBI text and writing down why one answer is
     right.
  3. The clause's source link, exact wording, and effective date are
     also checked.

This is called "dual annotation + adjudication" in the project docs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TWO REMAINING ITEMS (4 AND 5)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

These are the "small account" exemption questions: does a small account
(under paragraph 2's proviso in the RBI KYC Master Direction) get
exempted from paragraph 38(e)'s due-notice requirement?

Both annotator groups said yes (matching the draft hypothesis), but the
adjudication pass found a genuine ambiguity in the source text itself:

  - Paragraph 2's proviso ("Provided that this Direction shall not
    apply to 'small accounts'...") sits directly after sub-rule 2(b),
    which is about overseas branches and subsidiaries.
  - Reading 1: the proviso exempts small accounts from the ENTIRE
    Direction (including paragraph 38(e)). This is what both annotator
    groups and the draft hypothesis assumed.
  - Reading 2: the proviso exempts small accounts only from the
    immediately preceding sub-rule 2(b). This is the more
    grammatically natural reading given the proviso's position.

The obligation record already carried the project owner's own note
flagging this exact question before the adjudication pass found it
independently.

WHAT WOULD RESOLVE THIS: either an official RBI clarification, FAQ,
or circular that says which reading is correct, or a qualified legal
reviewer's opinion on the proviso's scope. Until then, items 4 and 5
stay at "dual_annotated" -- honestly uncertain rather than guessing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
CIRCULARITY CAVEAT (IMPORTANT FOR THE PAPER)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The 100% accuracy number (125/125) is real but must not be reported as
proof that the system works correctly on new, unseen regulatory
questions. The benchmark's facts, entity profiles, and expected
outcomes were originally generated from the same rule engine now being
evaluated. So "100% accuracy" means the resolver agrees with the data
it produced -- internal consistency, not proven real-world correctness.

Breaking this circularity requires benchmark items whose expected
outcomes are derived independently of the resolver (e.g., by a
qualified legal reviewer working from the primary source text alone,
without seeing the resolver's output).

This caveat is also documented in docs/ILLUSTRATIVE_EVAL.md (for the
earlier illustrative run) and in docs/DECISIONS.md D-009 (for this
real run).
